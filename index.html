Screen 01 Community Hub

Home Feed & Social Publishing

الصفحة الرئيسية والمنشورات

The central hub for every resident — a dynamic social feed unifying compound announcements, user posts, and media, with rapid shortcuts to essential services.

  • Quick Service Shortcuts: Instant access to maintenance, gate passes, housekeeping, and directory from top quick-actions.
  • Resident Post Creation: Share community updates, announcements, or inquiries with neighbors in one tap.
  • Multi-Media Gallery: Rich multi-photo carousel posts with swipe indicators and high-resolution viewing.
  • Role-Badged Engagement: Likes, comments, and shares featuring verified role badges (e.g. 'Owner') for trust.
