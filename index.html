Modern apartment buildings and gated compounds are held together by disconnected tools that cause frustration for residents, boards, and property staff.

Unmoderated group chats expose your private phone number to strangers, bury crucial building announcements under hundreds of casual messages, and allow departed tenants to linger forever.

WhatsUnity Solution: Verified unit directory without sharing phone numbers. Official announcements cleanly separated from social chats.

Lost Maintenance Requests

Verbal requests to janitors or lost texts to building supervisors result in delayed repairs, disputes over shared expenses, and zero progress tracking.

WhatsUnity Solution: 1-tap photo maintenance requests. Take a photo, submit in seconds, and track repair status from diagnosis to sign-off.

Visitor Gate Friction

Visitors wait at security gates while guards try calling residents over spotty cellular networks, creating long queues and security risks.

WhatsUnity Solution: 100% offline cryptographic QR visitor passes. Gatekeepers scan and verify passes in sub-50ms without internet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does “One home. One subscription. Everyone in your household included” mean?

WhatsUnity licenses per residential unit rather than per user. One subscription covers the entire household, allowing homeowners to invite family members with independent accounts at zero additional cost.

How does 100% offline gatekeeping work?

Visitor QR passes embed encrypted unit tokens and cryptographic timestamps. Gatekeeper tablets validate passes locally in under 50ms without needing an active cellular or Wi-Fi connection.

How does a residential building or compound get started?

Residents or HOA board members contact us via WhatsApp (+201158428601) or email (support@whatsunity.app). Compounds are onboarded with turnkey data setup within 48 hours.
